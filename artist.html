The artist

The artist who sees.

Art has been part of Sumanth’s life since his student years: pencil sketching, Tanjore painting, craftwork and digital art.

Placeholder images. These four pictures are examples by other artists, one per medium. They will be replaced with photographs of Sumanth’s own work.

Mediums

Pencil sketching

Line, tone and patience, drawing in graphite.

Tanjore painting

The classical South Indian tradition of rich colour and gold.

Craftwork

Making by hand, with paper and other materials.

Digital art

Drawing and composing on screen.

During his Second PUC he took part in an intercollegiate drawing and painting competition held at the Government College in his hometown, and his work has earned certificates, trophies and recognition. Award details and certificate photographs are still to be added.
